Riot API

I have been wanting to create my own way of analyzing my league data to be able to get greater insight to my games. Mostly I want to be able to see what team comps on my team and enemy team hurt or help my win rate. I also think this will be a good project to understand and explore the world of APIs. I know that APIs are ubiquitous in the world of programming and I want to be able to understand how they work and how to use them.
